Continuous telemetry from MQ Gas, Temp & Soil sensors.

2

4

5

CONFIRM

Architecture Highlights

Built for Physical Reality

Designed from the ground up to solve the real dangers of hallucinating AI in physical spaces.

100% Offline-First

Internet outages must never disable safety. Gas detection, valve shutoff, and audit logging execute on local silicon without a single packet leaving the device.

Dual-Brain Hardware

Arduino Uno sensor hub manages deterministic analog loops and autonomous valve emergency fallbacks, while ESP32 drives the OLED, RFID, and voice gateway.

Human-in-the-Loop

Physical operations require tactile confirmation. BAT POD calculates context, displays clear parameters, and waits for a physical touch before dispensing water.

Role-Based Access (RBAC)

Authenticated RFID identification separates Guests, Workers, and Admins. Strangers can query status but cannot command physical actuators.

Hardware Verification

Never declare success because a signal left the pin. Actuators confirm actual position before the voice synthesizes a confirmation report.

Explainable Incident Memory

Ask "What happened?" and BAT POD queries its SQLite log to accurately report who commanded what, what sensors detected, and why emergency procedures fired.

Frequently Asked Questions

Architecture, safety policies, and hardware setup details.

Can the LLM hallucinate and accidentally close or open the valve?

No. The LLM is strictly restricted to linguistic parsing and natural speech generation. It outputs structured intent data which is subsequently verified by hardcoded deterministic rules, an RBAC permission matrix, and an explicit human-in-the-loop approval mechanism.

What happens during a gas leak emergency?

When gas levels reach or exceed 300 PPM, BAT POD immediately triggers an emergency intervention. Unlike ordinary commands, emergency actions bypass human approval to close the valve servo in less than 1 second, sound the alarm, and log the incident to SQLite.

Can I test this without physical microcontrollers?

Yes! The built-in Hardware Abstraction Layer (HAL) includes high-fidelity mock adapters for every sensor, servo, RFID reader, and OLED screen. You can run the entire Golden Path demo in simulation mode directly from this web console or via CLI.

Does it support Indian languages?

Yes. BAT POD includes Kannada phrase mappings for speech synthesis and recognition, with support for Sarvam AI speech models when an API key is configured.
